# PFE: the goal, and what "done" means

Living file. Updated 24 Nov 2026.

## What we are making

A **mémoire / rapport de PFE** for the Faculté des Sciences, Université Ibn Tofail (Kénitra).

- **Title (hers):** Étude intégrée de la filière eau et boues : traitement biologique des eaux usées et valorisation énergétique des boues d'épuration
- **Problématique (hers):** Dans quelle mesure l'intégration du traitement biologique des eaux usées et de la valorisation énergétique des boues permet-elle d'améliorer la performance environnementale et énergétique d'une station d'épuration ?
- **Her introduction outline:** contexte et importance du traitement des eaux usées, problématique, hypothèses, objectifs, méthodologie générale, organisation du mémoire.
- **Sources she is allowed to use** (the photo): Google Scholar, PubMed, ScienceDirect, International Water Association (IWA). Keywords in English as well as French.

## The faculty's rules (from Pr. Rabea ZIRI's methodology course, Parts 1 and 2)

| Rule | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| 30 to 60 pages | Aim for about 45 pages of body, plus front matter and annexes |
| A4, Times New Roman 12, single spacing, page numbers centred at the bottom, recto only | Set in the Word template from the start |
| Cover page: title, nature of the report, author and contacts, institution name and logo, date | Logo and her details from her |
| Remerciements / dédicace | She writes these herself (they must sound like her) |
| Résumés in **French, English and Arabic**, half a page each, each with keywords | Three abstracts |
| Table des matières with continuous page numbers, annexes included | Generated by Word |
| Classic plan: Introduction, Synthèse bibliographique, Matériel et méthodes, Résultats et discussion, Conclusion, Références, Annexes | Our skeleton |
| Introduction about one tenth of the text: subject and importance, problématique, plan | Written last |
| Conclusion in two parts: **bilan** then **ouverture** | Past tenses for the bilan |
| Impersonal tone, no decoration | "On", passive voice, no "je" |
| Every figure and table has a legend and is commented in the text | |
| Every borrowed idea is cited in the text and in the bibliography | Anti-plagiarism is graded |
| References in **NF Z 44-005 / ISO 690**, alphabetical by author: `AUTEUR N. Année. Titre. Revue, vol., n°, pages.` | One consistent style |

## Proposed structure (to confirm against the audios and her encadrant)

1. Introduction générale (her outline above)
2. **Partie I: Synthèse bibliographique**
   - Ch. 1 Les eaux usées: origines, composition, paramètres (DBO5, DCO, MES, N, P), normes marocaines de rejet
   - Ch. 2 Le traitement biologique: boues activées, lagunage, biofiltres, MBBR/MBR; nitrification, dénitrification, déphosphatation
   - Ch. 3 Les boues d'épuration: production, caractérisation, épaississement, déshydratation, filières d'élimination
   - Ch. 4 La valorisation énergétique: digestion anaérobie, biogaz, cogénération, autres voies (incinération, pyrolyse, séchage solaire); bilan énergétique d'une STEP
3. **Partie II: Matériel et méthodes**: the station studied (or the case study chosen), data collected, analytical methods, how the environmental and energy performance is calculated (removal rates, kWh/m³, biogas yield, energy self-sufficiency, avoided CO₂)
4. **Partie III: Résultats et discussion**: water line performance, sludge line, energy balance with and without digestion, comparison with the literature and with Moroccan cases (e.g. the STEPs with digestion in Morocco), answer to each hypothesis
5. Conclusion générale (bilan + ouverture)
6. Références bibliographiques (real, checkable papers only)
7. Annexes

## What makes teachers give a high mark

- A problématique that is actually answered, hypothesis by hypothesis, in Part III.
- **Real data**: numbers from a real station (her internship, or published data from a named Moroccan STEP), not only general theory.
- Calculations shown step by step (energy balance, biogas, self-sufficiency rate), with units.
- Clean figures and tables made for this report, each commented.
- 30 to 50 real references, recent (mostly 2015 to 2026), from the allowed databases, cited correctly.
- Strict respect of the format rules above.
- She can defend every page orally: the soutenance decides a lot of the mark.
